Boost Your Electronic Design Reliability with Synthelyzer

Automated ECAD plugin for stress analysis, MTBF prediction, and component derating

Why Synthelyzer?

Synthelyzer empowers design engineers with an on-premise ECAD plugin that enhances design reliability from the schematic stage. It provides real-time stress analysis, component derating checks, and MTBF estimates before layout — reducing costly design changes and improving compliance.

Key Benefits

  • Detect electrical stress issues early
  • Reduce costly redesigns and routing changes
  • Support compliance (FMECA, MTBF, derating)
  • Enable faster, more informed design decisions
  • Ideal for aerospace, defense, automotive, and medical sectors

What Problems Does It Solve?

  • Component overstress and overdesign
  • Errors missed by standard DRC or schematic review tools
  • Reliance on outdated derating “thumb rules”
  • Compliance and documentation gaps
